# EMA/MACD one-change-at-a-time experiment — 2026-10-13

**Result: FAIL for all tested variants at assumed 0.50% round-trip cost.**
This is a *proxy* inspired by the public AlphaInsider Three EMAs research candidate, **not** a replication of its unreleased source strategy or trades. Not a forward test and not a trading authorization.

## Prespecified method
- Instrument: BTC-USD, ETH-USD; Coinbase Exchange public completed hourly OHLCV, UTC; independent of AlphaInsider API and MomentumFlow's private Alpaca data.
- Raw data: 2024-10-01 through 2026-10-12 inclusive (17,808 hourly bars per instrument, after disclosed gap fills).
- Evaluation with indicators warmed up from 2024-10-01:
  - **Older:** 2025-03-10 through 2025-10-12 (217 days)
  - **Newer holdout:** 2025-10-13 through 2026-10-12 (365 days)
- Long/cash only; no shorting, leverage or margin; only one position per instrument. Each backtest starts at 1.0 account equity. Reinvestment of entire notional when trading, with no position cap. No contemporaneous portfolio-level simulation across both symbols.
- Causal signal evaluation on the **last closed** 1-hour candle; hypothetical entry/exit at the **next hour open**. Indicators from historical bars through the signal bar only. Not tick-data or real execution.
- MACD(12,26,9) golden-cross entry, inverse cross exit.
- Incremental comparisons:
  1. MACD only
  2. MACD + price above hourly EMA200 at signal close
  3. #2 + price above hourly EMA20, with EMA20 above EMA200
  4. #3 + ATR14 stop at 2×ATR and take-profit at 4×ATR (2:1 target); MACD exit still applies. If stop and target hit within one candle, stop first.
- Conservative **assumed** per-side friction = 0.25% (0.50% round trip) by entry price increase and exit price decrease (fees + slippage combined). This is a sensitivity assumption, not verified real-world execution cost.
- Metrics: position-weighted compounded net return; maximum mark-to-market drawdown while open (with exit friction), completed round-trip count including end-of-window marks. Sample-size / volatility error bars not established.

## Data quality, reproducibility and caveats
- Public Coinbase Exchange /products/<symbol>/candles, granularity=3600. The backend must not require keys or place orders. Data fetched at time of run; API revisions are possible.
- 17,798 native candles + 10 missing hourly candles per symbol (17,808 after causal flat-fill).
- Both BTC and ETH data were missing exactly the same two **five-hour periods**, 2025-10-25 16:00–20:00 UTC and 2026-05-08 02:00–06:00 UTC. The script fills each missing hour with the **previous known** close (flat OHLC, zero volume), with strict failure if missing exceeds 0.1%. These are source outages; hourly strategy fills across them are approximations.
- Coinbase cached BTC file SHA-256: `c028f2f3fd2ed367f6a67d48bd9d864fb0f207cf58ec9391f4e5405918399125`.
- Coinbase cached ETH file SHA-256: `f6e74bc694cd8ca7ff19e78eeee5ae05cb0603236ed4c6f2cdd8911ce7a5219b`.
- Exact script: [ema_macd_ablation.py](../ema_macd_ablation.py); automated correctness tests: [test_ema_macd_ablation.py](../test_ema_macd_ablation.py). Run:
  ```bash
  python3 momentumflow/research/ema_macd_ablation.py --cache-dir /tmp/ema-macd-research
  python3 -m unittest discover -s momentumflow/research -p 'test_ema_macd_ablation.py' -v
  ```
- Five unit tests pass for next-open execution, EMA warmup, indicator filters, friction, and stop-before-target collision. Live/paper bot unchanged.
- No claim of causal inference that filters *cause* profit; these are comparisons with the same inputs. Zero-cost tests are diagnostics, not executable net profitability.

## Lessons retained
1. Hourly MACD signals triggered many trades, often with too little average edge to cover 0.25% per-side assumed friction.
2. EMA200 then EMA20 each reduced trading frequency and loss for BTC/ETH in both windows, but **did not produce a positive net result** at the cost assumption.
3. Adding a fixed 2ATR stop and 4ATR target to this specific setup reduced observed net performance compared with exit on MACD alone.
4. The earlier AlphaInsider Three EMAs virtual strategy has fewer trade events and is not equivalent to this hourly proxy.
5. This run is a rejected research branch/baseline—not a license to rewrite, activate, or reconfigure any existing V51, V26, V35, MACD, or other MomentumFlow runtime.

## Next hypotheses (not yet tested)
- Examine fewer, higher-quality entry signals without arbitrarily requiring the same frequent cross sequence (e.g., MACD turn/depth with price/volume trigger). Keep trade-count targets separate from profit targets.
- Test friction calibrated to real available broker/exchange quote and execution conditions, not assumed zero cost; validate on *new* untouched periods and at least three independently sourced markets.
- Examine gaps by cross-checking alternative vendor candles before execution-facing work.
